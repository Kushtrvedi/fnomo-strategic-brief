Fnomo -- Go-To-Market Execution Strategy
Prepared by: Kush Trivedi
Fnomo is not entering the market as another fintech product competing with brokers, apps, or content platforms. It sits earlier in the journey. Today, users can discover options, read content, and execute transactions easily. What they still lack is clarity at the moment they are about to act. That gap is where most hesitation, mistakes, and regret happen. Fnomo is positioned to fill that gap. It starts as a discovery and comparison layer and evolves into a system where users can validate their decisions before taking action. The goal is simple: when a user is about to make a financial or high-value decision, Fnomo becomes the place they check before proceeding.
The GTM strategy is built around where real decisions happen, not where attention is highest. Instead of broad marketing or heavy paid acquisition, the focus is on entering environments where users are already thinking about decisions:
 Communities where discussions and validation happen  Platforms where users compare options  High-frequency decision environments such as trading and purchases This allows Fnomo to reach users at high-intent moments, leading to better activation and lower acquisition costs.
Execution follows a bottom-up approach. The initial focus is on communities, traders, and active decision-makers who already operate in these environments. This allows for faster feedback, quicker iteration, and early validation of behavior.

Once usage patterns are validated, distribution expands to influencers, fintech platforms, and institutional partnerships.
Performance is tracked through one primary metric: Pre-action usage rate -- the percentage of users who use Fnomo before making a decision. Supporting metrics include activation, retention, and channel-level acquisition cost, with a strong focus on keeping CAC efficient and improving repeat usage.
Revenue is built through partnerships, not direct monetization at the start. Fnomo aligns with:
 Influencers  Communities  Fintech platforms through revenue-sharing models, ensuring low upfront cost and aligned incentives.
What makes this approach work is positioning. Fnomo does not compete with existing platforms. It sits before them. It does not try to capture attention. It captures behavior at the point where decisions are made. If executed correctly, this creates a simple habit: Before making a decision  check Fnomo  then act
The strategy is designed to be executed immediately, even in a lean setup, with direct ownership of outreach, feedback loops, and early partnerships. The focus is not on scale from day one, but on validating behavior first and then expanding distribution based on what works.

In short, Fnomo is not trying to be another platform in the ecosystem. It is positioning itself as the layer that influences decisions before the ecosystem is used
