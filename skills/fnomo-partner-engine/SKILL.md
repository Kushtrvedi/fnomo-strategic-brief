# FNOMO Partner Engine

Partners are FNOMO's distribution engine. A well-managed partner base compounds — each CA
refers other CAs, each influencer's audience becomes a warm acquisition channel. This skill
manages everything from first onboarding to strategic tier close.

---

## Partner Tier Structure

| Tier | Name | Rev Share | Who Gets It |
|------|------|-----------|-------------|
| Entry | Standard Partner | 20–30% | New onboards — first 2 weeks |
| Performing | Active Partner | 30–40% | Partners generating consistent activations |
| Strategic | Strategic Partner | Up to 50% | Top 1 partner — 90-day commitment |

**Upgrade rules**:
- Entry → Performing: After 10+ activations generated, or after 2 weeks of consistent referrals
- Performing → Strategic: Reserve for one partner only — the one with the highest distribution + trust

**40% Flexibility Cap Rule**: Do not default to maximum rev share on first conversation.
Use the high end (40–50%) as an entry lever only for strategic closes or partners with outsized distribution.
Start at the lower end of the range and upgrade based on performance.

## Partner Types

### Type A: Chartered Accountants (Advisors)
- Distribution engine: 50–500 clients each
- Referral model: Client uses FNOMO before a financial decision → CA earns 20–30% rev share
- How to activate: WhatsApp call → live demo → referral link setup
- Retention: Weekly earnings updates, upgrade notifications, co-branded output (their name on decision checks)

### Type B: Finance Influencers / Creators
- Distribution engine: 10K–500K audience members
- Referral model: Creator mentions FNOMO at a decision moment → audience activates → creator earns 10–25%
- How to activate: Call → screen share demo card → provide 1-liner they can use in content
- Retention: Earnings updates, social proof from other creators, content integration examples
- Note: Rev share is negotiated per creator — use audience size, engagement rate, and niche relevance
  to determine entry rate. Start at 10% for micro-creators, up to 25% for 200K+ engaged audiences.

### Type C: Fintech / Platform Partners
- Distribution engine: 10K–1M+ users per platform
- Referral model: Platform integrates FNOMO at decision moments → earns 5–15% of revenue from their users
- Targets: INDmoney, Smallcase, MoneyControl, ET Markets
- How to activate: BD conversation → pilot proposal → data-sharing agreement → integration
- Retention: Monthly performance data, product roadmap access, joint marketing opportunities

### Type D: Community Admins (Telegram/Reddit/TradingView)
- Distribution engine: 1K–50K community members per admin
- Referral model: Admin allows FNOMO to engage community → earns 10–20% rev share on conversions from their community
- How to activate: Permission to participate in community → organic decision responses → earned credibility → rev share proposal
- Retention: Community growth support, early access to product features, performance data

## Partner Onboarding System

### CA Onboarding Call Flow (20–30 min)

**Opening** (never lead with the product):
```
"Before I show you anything — when a client calls you before a major financial decision,
what's the one uncertainty they bring that you wish you had a cleaner framework for?"
[Let them answer — this defines your positioning for everything that follows]
```

**Demo** (after they describe the gap):
```
"That's exactly what FNOMO addresses. Let me show you what it looks like in practice —
I'll run a decision check on a scenario relevant to your client type."
[Run live manual MVP on their client niche — HNI, retail, NRI, etc.]
```

**Partner proposal**:
```
"Here's what I'm offering advisors who want to use this with their clients:

You get a referral link with your name on it. When a client uses FNOMO through your link
before a financial decision, you earn 20–30% of what we earn from that interaction.

Zero upfront cost. Zero pitch required — just use it naturally when they call with a
decision question. Want me to set up your link today?"
```

**Close**:
```
"I can have your link ready in 5 minutes. Try it with your next 5 client queries —
no commitment, no formal pitch to clients. If it adds value, we formalise. If not, nothing lost.
Want to start this week?"
```

### Influencer Onboarding Call Flow (15 min)

**Demo**:
```
[Screen share the decision demo card]
"This is what your audience gets when they check FNOMO before acting on your content.
Score, Risk, Alternative — 10 seconds. The integration is zero-friction: you mention
FNOMO at the decision moment, not as a sponsor, but as a tool you actually use."
```

**The ask**:
```
"Here's the simplest structure: mention FNOMO at one decision moment in your next video
or post. I'll handle everything — the link, the tracking, the user experience.
You earn [X]% per user who activates. No minimum, no contract.

Want me to send you the link and a 1-liner you can use in your next piece of content?"
```

### Fintech/Platform Onboarding (First Meeting)

**Opening**:
```
"I want to explore one specific moment on your platform — the second before a user
completes a purchase or executes a trade. FNOMO is a decision validation check that
lives in that moment. No competing with your core flow — we add confidence to the
action your users are already about to take."
```

**Pilot proposal**:
```
"I'm proposing a 30-day pilot — lightweight integration or side-by-side test with
50–100 users. At the end, you have data on whether pre-decision validation reduces
regret and improves user satisfaction. No commitment beyond the pilot."
```

## Referral Chain Activation (Asking CAs for Referrals)

Only activate this after the CA has had at least 2 weeks of engagement and has seen client usage.

```
Quick ask — your clients have been using FNOMO well this week. Are there 1–2 other
advisors or CAs in your network you think would find this useful for their clients?

Even just a forward of this message with a note would be amazing. I'll handle
everything from there.
```

**For each referred contact received**:
- Message within 24 hours
- Open with: "Hi [Name] — [Referring CA's name] suggested I reach out."
- Same onboarding script as standard CA outreach

## Strategic Partner Close (Up to 50% Rev Share)

This is the single highest-value close in the playbook. Reserve for one partner only.
Only attempt after 3+ weeks of data to identify the right person.

**How to identify the strategic partner**:
- Highest activations generated
- Largest activated audience
- Highest trust with their audience (engagement rate, not just followers)
- Willingness to make FNOMO their primary decision recommendation

**The call opener**:
```
"You've been our strongest partner over the last [X] weeks. Your [community/clients/audience]
has engaged better than anyone else we're working with.

I want to offer you something I'm not offering broadly — the strategic tier. Up to 50% rev share,
your name co-branded on decision outputs, and first access to everything we build next.

The ask: FNOMO becomes your go-to decision recommendation for the next 90 days.
Can we make that official today?"
```

**The close**:
```
"I have one strategic partnership slot open. I want it to be you.

Can we formalize this today? I can have the rev share agreement on WhatsApp within the hour —
it's simple, one page, no legal complexity. Just our names and the structure."
```
