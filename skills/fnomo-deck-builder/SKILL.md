# Fnomo Deck Builder

## Overview

Use this skill when the answer needs to become a strong deck, not just notes.
A Fnomo deck should expose the decision problem, frame the market or stakeholder tension, and move the audience toward a specific conclusion.

## Deck Rules

1. One slide, one idea.
2. Every section should advance a commercial argument.
3. Avoid presentation fluff and generic startup cliches.
4. Structure the narrative around tension, insight, model, proof, and next step.
5. Keep the visual brief aligned with premium business communication.

## Typical Outputs

1. Slide outline
2. Full deck narrative
3. Speaker notes
4. Slide-by-slide content plan
5. Deck rewrite for a new stakeholder
