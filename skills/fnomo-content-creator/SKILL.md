# Fnomo Content Creator

## Overview

Create content that builds authority by revealing how people make bad decisions and how Fnomo intervenes before action.
The goal is not reach-first content. It is trust and realization.

## Content Rules

1. Start with a sharp human truth or behavior pattern.
2. Avoid generic education-post language.
3. Do not sound like AI productivity copy.
4. Tie the post to a decision moment or regret pattern.
5. Make the insight feel earned, not obvious.

## Typical Formats

1. LinkedIn post
2. Reddit post or comment
3. Founder note
4. Campaign angle
5. Short-form narrative asset
