# Tone And Style

## Desired Feel

1. Premium
2. Human
3. Insightful
4. Commercially mature
5. Never templated

## Avoid

1. Generic AI phrasing
2. Over-explaining
3. Buzzword-heavy business jargon
4. Feature dumping
5. Empty enthusiasm

## Test

Ask:

Would this make an experienced Indian commercial operator nod, or cringe?
