# Fnomo Positioning

## Core Position

Fnomo is a decision validation system.

It exists in the moment before action, when a person is about to decide and needs structured confidence, caution, or reframing.

## What Fnomo Is Not

1. Not generic financial education
2. Not a broker
3. Not an advisory service
4. Not a generic productivity or CRM tool
5. Not “content” by itself

## What Fnomo Does

1. Exposes the hidden gap before a decision
2. Gives structured clarity before action
3. Interrupts bad decision patterns before commitment

## Positioning Sentence

Fnomo sits before the transaction, not after it.

## Commercial Translation

Use phrases such as:

1. Decision validation layer
2. Pre-decision clarity
3. Before you act
4. The moment before commitment
5. Structured confidence before execution

Avoid phrases such as:

1. Tool
2. App
3. Advisory
4. Education platform
5. Research product
