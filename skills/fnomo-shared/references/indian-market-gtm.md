# Indian Market GTM Notes

## Tone

1. Premium, direct, respectful
2. Commercially intelligent, not over-polished
3. Human and grounded, never mass-message style

## Behavioral Reality

1. Trust is earned through relevance, not claims
2. Warm introductions and contextual references matter
3. Founder-quality thinking beats agency-style fluff
4. Many prospects are cautious about sounding sold to

## Practical Rule

Every message should feel like it came from a peer who understands their commercial reality.
