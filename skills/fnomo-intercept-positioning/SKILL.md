# FNOMO Intercept Positioning

The Intercept Blueprint is FNOMO's core philosophy. Every pitch, every partnership conversation,
every community response is an expression of this single idea:

**"Capture behavior at the point of decision, not attention at the point of discovery."**

---

## The Core Thesis (Know This Cold)

Every financial tool in India does one of two things:
1. Helps users **research** (MoneyControl, Screener, ET Markets, YouTube creators)
2. Helps users **execute** (Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Paytm Money)

**No one owns the moment between research and execution.**

That moment — when the user has done their research, has their conclusion, but hasn't tapped
"Buy" yet — is where confidence is lowest and regret is highest.

FNOMO owns that moment. That is The Intercept.

---

## The Regret & Hesitation Curve

Explain this to any stakeholder with the following narrative:

```
"Imagine a user who has done 2 hours of research on a stock. They've read the analysis,
watched the YouTube videos, checked the charts. They're at their broker app, finger on the
Buy button.

That's when confidence crashes. That's the hesitation moment.

Studies show this is when most regret-generating decisions happen — either they freeze and
miss the right move, or they rush and make the wrong one.

FNOMO is the validation check that lives in that 10 seconds. Score. Risk. Alternative.
Not advice. Not analysis. Just structured confidence."
```

**The Timing-Based Differentiation diagram** (describe verbally):
- Left side: Aggregators (research phase) — MoneyControl, ET Markets, Screener
- Right side: Execution platforms (action phase) — Zerodha, Groww, Upstox
- **Center gap — FNOMO**: The validation slot between research and execution

FNOMO does not compete with anyone on either side. It completes the user journey.

## What FNOMO Is and Is Not

### IS:
- A decision validation system
- The layer where users compare and validate before they decide
- A pre-decision confidence check (Score/Risk/Alternative)
- A behavior capture mechanism at the highest-intent moment in the user's journey
- A system that gives decision clarity at the moment of action

### IS NOT:
- A content platform (doesn't compete with YouTube creators or financial media)
- A research tool (doesn't compete with Screener, MoneyControl)
- A financial advisor (not SEBI-regulated, not giving recommendations)
- A portfolio tracker (doesn't compete with INDmoney, Kuvera)
- An execution platform (doesn't compete with Zerodha, Groww)
- An app or tool (it's a system, a layer, a decision engine)

## The Intercept Habit Loop

This is the North Star behavior FNOMO is building:

```
Decision Triggered
       ↓
  Check FNOMO (10 seconds)
       ↓
  Confident Action Taken
       ↓
  Monetization (rev share back to ecosystem)
       ↓
  [User checks FNOMO before NEXT decision]
       ↓
  Loop compounds
```

The goal is to make "check FNOMO before acting" as automatic as checking Google Maps before driving.
Not because it's required — because the outcome is always better when you do.

**North Star Metric**: Pre-Action Usage Rate — percentage of users who check FNOMO before
executing a financial decision.
- Month 1 Target: 20%
- Month 2 Target: 30%
- Month 3 Target: 40%+

## The Three V1 Decision Engines (What the Product Actually Does)

When asked "what does it actually do?" — explain all three:

**1. Investment Decision Engine**
Input: Stock/fund/asset + amount + horizon
Output: Decision Score (1–10), Risk Level, Reason (specific data), Alternative

**2. Purchase Decision Engine**
Input: Product + price + EMI/cash
Output: Affordability Score, Regret Probability, Smarter Alternative

**3. Trade Decision Engine**
Input: Trade idea + entry/target/stop
Output: Risk Heat (🟢🟡🔴⚫), Probability Band, Exposure Warning

All three engines share the same design principle: **decision clarity in under 10 seconds.**
No dashboards. No complex analytics. No jargon. One structured output, immediately actionable.
