# Fnomo GTM Strategy

## Overview

Use this skill for strategic GTM judgment with execution consequences.
Its job is to convert market context into clear commercial choices.

## Strategy Rules

1. Start from decision moments, not awareness fantasies.
2. Rank channels by conviction, not novelty.
3. Tie every strategic recommendation to an execution implication.
4. Name trade-offs explicitly.
5. Keep the answer Indian-market-aware and commercially realistic.

## Output Format

Return:

1. Strategic question
2. Decision gap in the market
3. Options
4. Recommended choice
5. Why this wins
6. Execution implication
