# Fnomo Partnership Builder

## Overview

Use this skill for partner-led distribution.
Treat every partnership as a trust-and-distribution engine, not just a logo acquisition.

## Rules

1. Lead with why the partner's audience or ecosystem has a decision gap.
2. Explain how Fnomo fits before the transaction, not after it.
3. Avoid over-offering on rev share before fit is clear.
4. Keep onboarding simple and concrete.
5. When the partner is warm, move toward a defined next step quickly.

## Typical Outputs

1. Partner outreach message
2. Partnership call structure
3. Revenue-share framing
4. Partner update
5. Partner escalation or reactivation move
