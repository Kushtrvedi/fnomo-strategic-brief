## The Core Thesis (Know This Cold)

Every financial tool in India does one of two things:
1. Helps users **research** — MoneyControl, Screener, ET Markets, YouTube creators
2. Helps users **execute** — Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Paytm Money

**No one owns the moment between research and execution.**

That moment — when the user has done their homework, has their conclusion, but hasn't tapped "Buy" yet — is where confidence is lowest and regret is highest.

**FNOMO owns that moment. That is The Intercept.**

## The Three V1 Decision Engines

When asked "what does it actually do?":

**1. Investment Decision Engine**
Input: Stock/fund/asset + amount + horizon
Output: Decision Score (1–10), Risk Level, Reason (specific data), Alternative

**2. Purchase Decision Engine**
Input: Product + price + EMI/cash
Output: Affordability Score, Regret Probability, Smarter Alternative

**3. Trade Decision Engine**
Input: Trade idea + entry/target/stop
Output: Risk Heat (🟢🟡🔴⚫), Probability Band, Exposure Warning

All three engines share one design principle: **decision clarity in under 10 seconds.**
No dashboards. No complex analytics. No jargon. One structured output, immediately actionable.

## Workflow Process

### Phase 1: Identify the Stakeholder
1. Who are you pitching to? (CA / influencer / corporate / platform / investor / community member)
2. What do they care about? (clients, audience, employees, users, returns)
3. What objection are they most likely to raise?

### Phase 2: Select the Right Angle
1. Lead with the stakeholder's world (their clients' hesitation, their audience's freeze, their employees' stress)
2. Bridge to The Intercept (the gap between research and execution)
3. Land on FNOMO's role (the 10-second validation check)

### Phase 3: Handle Competitive Questions
1. Acknowledge the comparison (don't dismiss it)
2. Explain the timing-based differentiation clearly
3. Use the "we complete, not compete" frame

### Phase 4: Close the Positioning
1. Always end with what FNOMO is building toward (the habit loop, the Pre-Action Usage Rate)
2. Never leave the stakeholder uncertain about what FNOMO does in one sentence: "Decision clarity before action."
