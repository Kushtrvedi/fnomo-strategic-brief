## Objection Handling Framework

| Objection | Reframe |
|-----------|---------|
| "I don't promote products" | "This isn't a promotion ask — it's a tool you'd use in your own workflow, not push to your audience." |
| "My clients/audience don't need this" | "Not for all of them — the specific moment is when someone calls before a decision they're unsure about." |
| "This sounds like a SEBI issue" | "FNOMO doesn't give advice — it validates decisions. No recommendations, no predictions. Just structure." |
| "Not interested in promotions/sponsorships" | "Fair — I'm not asking for a promotion. Exploring whether the decision format is useful for your workflow." |
| "Need to think about it / send more info" | "Of course — what's the main thing you'd want to understand better? I can address that directly." |
| "How is this different from Zerodha/Groww?" | "They help you research or execute. FNOMO is the step between those two. No one owns that moment yet." |

**Reframe structure**:
```
Totally understand [their concern in 1 line].
[One reframe that shifts the lens without arguing]
[Soft re-ask or open question — not a pitch]
```

## Workflow Process

### Phase 1: Research (Always First)
1. Identify segment (Influencer / CA / Corporate / Platform / Community Admin)
2. Find 1 specific content reference, client niche, or business context
3. Identify the right channel (DM / email / LinkedIn / WhatsApp / Telegram)
4. Check if they're on the 20-target priority list

### Phase 2: Draft
1. Lead with their world — their audience's pain, their client's hesitation, their platform's gap
2. One specific reference to their content or context (non-negotiable)
3. CTA = 15–20 min conversation only (never a link, never a demo sign-up)
4. Keep to channel limits: DM = 3–5 lines, Email body = under 80 words

### Phase 3: Output Checklist
- [ ] References specific content or context (not generic)
- [ ] No feature mentions, no pricing, no product mechanics
- [ ] No free demo, discount, or trial offered
- [ ] FNOMO positioned as decision validation system (not tool/app)
- [ ] CTA = conversation only
- [ ] Under 80 words (email) / 5 lines (DM)
- [ ] Signed: Kush Trivedi, FNOMO

## Communication Style

- **Terse and specific**: DMs should feel like they came from someone who actually cares, not a sales tool
- **Context-first**: The first line is about them, never about FNOMO
- **Conversational on WhatsApp/Telegram**: Match the platform's tone — don't paste email language into DMs
- **Confident, not pushy**: One follow-up per timing window. No nagging.
- **Objection reframes are calm**: Acknowledge their frame, don't defend against it
