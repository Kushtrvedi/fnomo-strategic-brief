Founders advocate only after they experience a shift in how they think before making decisions. The 30-day job is usage -> realization -> proof -> advocacy -> growth.

# Customized 7-Day Paths

| Archetype | Day 1 Ask | Day 2-3 Tool | Day 4-5 Action | Day 6-7 Action | Metrics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mega Connector | Ask founder to share one thought: why most investors make mistakes despite good information. | Give LinkedIn post template: mistakes feel informed because inputs are not the same as decision validation. | Ask founder to publish post and reply to comments with the decision-validation frame. | Ask founder to DM 2-3 engaged commenters and open founder conversations. | Post published, 20+ comments, 3 inbound conversations, 2 sales by Day 14 |
| Trusted Advisor | Ask: Which client recently made a decision that did not work? | Give 1:1 script: Before you act on this, have you validated the decision? | Ask advisor to use the validation prompt with 3 clients. | Ask advisor to introduce Fnomo to 2 relevant clients. | 3 client conversations, 2 intros, 1 conversion by Day 7, 3 conversions by Day 14 |
| Operator Investor | Ask: What decision are you evaluating this week? | Ask founder to use Fnomo personally on the current decision. | Give WhatsApp share: I have started validating decisions before investing - makes a difference. | Ask founder to share privately with 2-3 peers. | 1 usage, 2 shares, 1 referral by Day 7, 3 referrals by Day 14 |
| Content Amplifier | Ask: What is the biggest mistake investors make? | Give video script: Most investors think they need better stock ideas, but the real problem is not validating decisions before acting. | Ask founder to create 1 reel or video. | Ask founder to post and engage comments for founder conversations. | 1 content piece, 1k+ views, 5 DMs by Day 7, 2 conversions by Day 14 |
